# Koa’s Events — Remaining Proposed Business Rules

Status: **PARTIALLY APPROVED**

The following recommendations were approved on 2026-09-17 and have moved into the active source of truth:

- Venue maximum occupancy: **100 persons**.
- Mobile Bar commercial pricing and operating rules were superseded on 2026-10-10 by the synchronized KoaSMobileBar.com rules: **$1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500 packages, $65/hour/bartender labor, 10% tip-jar or 25% no-tip-jar gratuity, package-specific guest surcharges and the published travel rules**.

## Recommendations still pending approval

### Public wedding pricing
Continue to show Gardenia, Orchid, Hibiscus and Plumeria as **starting at** prices rather than unconditional all-in totals.

### Package architecture
Keep the four package names but separate:
1. venue maximum occupancy,
2. included furniture/inventory,
3. access hours/days,
4. included services,
5. optional add-ons,
6. custom-quote requirements.

### Mobile Bar commercial rules
The Mobile Bar pricing, guest scaling, labor, gratuity and travel rules are commercially locked in `docs/business-rules-source-of-truth.md` and should no longer be treated as pending recommendations.

### Add-on pricing
Publish fixed prices only for standardized items with stable costs; use “from” or quote-only pricing for custom fabrication, décor and personalized goods.

### Kamaʻāina
Prefer a targeted resident benefit (weekday/off-peak pricing or a standardized upgrade) over a permanent blanket discount on prime dates.

### Reservation retainer
Current documents remain at a 10% non-refundable deposit. Consider 25% only for future bookings after all contract, invoice and welcome materials are updated together.

### 75 dB rule
The Event Day Checklist currently states music must remain under 75 dB. If this is a true venue rule, add it to the controlling agreement and Venue FAQ; otherwise remove it from the checklist.
