Status: **working control document for the website rebuild**

The purpose of this file is to stop pricing, capacity, package inclusions and policies from drifting between the website, inquiry forms, contracts and client documents.

## Status definitions

- **CONFIRMED** — supported consistently by current client/legal documents and safe to build into the site.
- **PUBLISHED CURRENT** — currently published on koasevents.com and used for the redesign, but still needs final owner approval before production launch.
- **CONFLICT** — two active sources disagree. Do not present as a master fact until resolved.
- **NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION** — no reliable controlling source yet.
