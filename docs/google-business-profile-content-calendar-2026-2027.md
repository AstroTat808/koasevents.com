# Koa’s Events — 36-Week Google Business Profile Content Calendar

**Cadence:** 2 posts per week (Tuesday + Friday)  
**Period:** 2026-09-22 through 2027-05-28  
**Primary objective:** strengthen local wedding-venue relevance, showcase the real property and guest experience, support Mobile Bar discovery, and turn Google Business Profile views into website visits and inquiries.

## Publishing rules

- Use the listed Koa’s first-party image asset; do not substitute stock photography.
- Keep the location language factual: Koa’s is in Mountain View, serving couples comparing Hilo, Volcano, East Hawaiʻi and Big Island options.
- Use one clear CTA per post.
- Do not repeat phone numbers, addresses or keyword lists in every post.
- Rotate destination URLs so GBP traffic lands on the page that actually answers the post topic.
- Before publishing an image, confirm its current gallery focal point/crop so the GBP version matches the website’s approved presentation.
- Review GBP Insights monthly and adjust future post topics toward the posts producing website clicks, calls and direction requests.

## Monthly content mix target

Across each four-week period, aim for roughly:
- 3–4 venue/property posts
- 2–3 real-wedding or ceremony/reception posts
- 2 Mobile Bar posts
- 1–2 package/pricing posts
- 1 private or corporate event post
- 1 cottage/stay or hospitality post
- 1 event-rental/guest-experience post
- 1–2 educational blog posts
- 1 direct inquiry/availability CTA

## Measurement

Track the following monthly in Google Business Profile:
1. Search impressions vs. Maps impressions.
2. Website clicks.
3. Call clicks.
4. Direction requests.
5. Search terms that repeatedly surface the profile.
6. Which content pillars coincide with increases in discovery for wedding venue, Hilo venue, Big Island wedding venue and Mobile Bar terms.

Do not judge individual posts only by visible engagement. GBP posts also support profile freshness, merchandising and conversion for people who arrive through Search or Maps and never interact with the post itself.
