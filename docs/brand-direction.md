# Koa’s Events — Brand Direction v1

Status: working direction for redesign; final logo artwork not yet selected.

## Brand idea

Modern Hawaiʻi hospitality for meaningful gatherings.

Koa’s should feel intimate, capable, warm and rooted in place. The brand should bridge two experiences without looking fragmented: a private Mountain View venue and a polished mobile bar that travels across Hawaiʻi Island.

## Positioning

Koa’s Events is for hosts who want a celebration that feels personal rather than processed. The value is not only the physical venue or the bar trailer; it is the combination of thoughtful hosting, practical event knowledge, flexible service and a distinctly Big Island sense of place.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. Master promise: Big Island celebrations, beautifully hosted.
2. Venue: Host at Koa’s.
3. Mobile Bar: Bring Koa’s to you.
4. Planning CTA: Tell us what you’re gathering for.

## Visual personality

- Refined, not formal.
- Tropical through material and landscape, not clichés.
- Editorial photography with human warmth.
- Generous negative space.
- Deep rainforest tones + warm natural accents.
- Avoid stock hibiscus/tiki/wave motifs, neon tropical palettes and overly rustic wedding styling.

## Provisional palette

## Typography direction

Display typography should feel editorial and organic; body typography should be exceptionally readable and modern. The homepage currently uses high-quality system stacks while the final brand font licensing and loading strategy are decided.

Recommended final pairing direction: characterful old-style/editorial serif + restrained geometric/humanist sans.

## Logo system brief

The final system should include:

- Primary Koa’s Events wordmark.
- Compact symbol for favicon/social/avatar use.
- Horizontal and stacked lockups.
- One-color versions for signage, embroidery and glass/vinyl applications.
- Service lockup for Koa’s Mobile Bar that is clearly part of the same master brand.

### Concepts to explore visually

1. Gathering Circle — an abstract mark combining a koa leaf silhouette with an open circle/gathering form.
2. Rainforest Monogram — a restrained K / KE monogram with organic negative space; premium but not resort-like.
3. Pavilion + Canopy — a simplified architectural/landscape mark referencing shelter, canopy and gathering without becoming a literal venue illustration.

The homepage uses a temporary text/initials lockup only. No provisional symbol should become the final logo by accident.
